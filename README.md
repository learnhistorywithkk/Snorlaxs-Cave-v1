<p id="mwCw"><b id="mwDA">Trollface</b> or <b id="mwDQ">Troll Face</b> is a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rage_comic" title="Rage comic" id="mwDg">rage comic</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meme" title="Meme" id="mwDw">meme</a> image of a character donning a mischievous smile, used to symbolize <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_troll" title="Internet troll" class="mw-redirect" id="mwEA">internet trolls</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolling" title="Trolling" id="mwEQ">trolling</a>. It is one of the oldest and most widely known rage comic faces.<sup about="#mwt10" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-1" id="mwEg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwEw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFA">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFQ">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt13" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Connor_2-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Connor"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-Connor-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Connor-2" id="mwFg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwFw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGA">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="2" id="mwVg" aria-labelledby="Usage"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Usage">Usage</h2></div>
<p id="mwVw">Trollface shows an internet troll, someone who annoys others on the internet for their own amusement.<sup about="#mwt37" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Connor_2-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Connor"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Connor-2" id="mwWA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwWQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwWg">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwWw">]</span></span></a></sup> The original comic by Ramirez mocked trolls;<sup about="#mwt38" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Klepek_3-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Klepek"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Klepek-3" id="mwXA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwXQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXg">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXw">]</span></span></a></sup> however, the image is widely used by those who troll.<sup about="#mwt41" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Macale_9-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Macale"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-Macale-9"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Macale-9" id="mwYA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwYQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwYg">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwYw">]</span></span></a></sup> Trollface has been described as the internet equivalent of the children's taunt "<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyah_nyah_nyah_nyah_nyah_nyah" title="Nyah nyah nyah nyah nyah nyah" id="mwZA">nyah nyah nyah nyah nyah nyah</a>" or <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongue#Gestures" title="Tongue" id="mwZQ">sticking one's tongue out</a>.<sup about="#mwt42" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Macale_9-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Macale"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Macale-9" id="mwZg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwZw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaA">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="3" id="mwag" aria-labelledby="Copyright"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Copyright">Copyright</h2></div>
<p id="mwaw">On April 8, 2015, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotaku" title="Kotaku" id="mwbA">Kotaku</a> ran an in-depth interview article with Ramirez about his now-iconic rage comic character. Copyright Office" id="mwbg">United States Copyright Office</a> on July 27, 2010, he had earned more than $100,000 in licensing fees and other payouts associated with Trollface, including from licensing for shirts emblazoned with the face being sold by the retail chain <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Topic" title="Hot Topic" id="mwbw">Hot Topic</a>, with monthly revenues reaching as high as $15,000 at its peak.<sup about="#mwt43" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Klepek_3-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref"
